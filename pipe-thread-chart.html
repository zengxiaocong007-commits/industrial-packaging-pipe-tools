Compatibility Warning: Never cross-mate 1/2" or 3/4" NPT (14 TPI, 60° crests) with BSPT (14 TPI, 55° rounded crests). Even though TPI matches at 1/2" and 3/4", the flank angle mismatch causes spiral leak paths under hydraulic or compressed air pressure.
